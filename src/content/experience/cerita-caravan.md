# Cerita Caravan

Cerita Caravan was a cross-campus collaboration and training program held from December 2024 to January 2025. As a **Volunteer & Storytelling Trainee**, I supported the event team while developing practical narrative communication skills.

## Key Contributions

- **Logistics Coordination:** Assisted event instructors in managing coordination for 50+ participants and securing necessary workshop equipment throughout the cross-campus training program.
- **Communication Training:** Actively engaged with mentors and participants to master advanced narrative communication and user-centric storytelling methodologies.
