# Design Thinking Project: Food Stock Management App

## 📌 Introduction
This project focuses on applying the Design Thinking methodology to develop a digital solution in the form of a household food stock management application.

## 🎯 Objective
To design an interactive prototype that can assist household users in managing their food supplies more intelligently, aiming to significantly reduce food waste.

## 💡 Background / Why It Exists
The high rate of food waste at the household level in Indonesia is a major barrier to achieving the SDG target (Goal 2: Zero Hunger). From our initial research, many people struggle to track the expiration dates of their groceries and have a habit of buying more than the necessary portions.

## 🧑‍💼 My Role
As a **UX Researcher & Product Designer**, I was responsible for conducting in-depth user research, formulating the Value Proposition Canvas, and translating these concepts into UI/UX designs and interactive prototypes using Figma.

## ⚙️ Modules Created
Through the Design Thinking iterations, my team and I developed the following research modules and prototype features:
*   **User Research Module:** This included creating questionnaires, establishing User Personas, and identifying the main Pain Points behind food-wasting habits.
*   **Value Proposition Mapping:** Designing the Value Proposition Canvas to ensure the app features accurately addressed user problems.
*   **Dashboard & Stock Report Design:** The main interface that provides a visual summary of the food inventory status.
*   **Analyze Stock Needs Feature:** A smart calculator that processes variables like duration, activity level, and number of people to provide precise grocery recommendations.
*   **Interactive AI Chatbot:** A virtual assistant prototype that caters to specific diet consultations and can recommend relevant ingredients straight to the user's cart.

## 🚀 Impact
This project successfully produced a high-fidelity app prototype that validated the concept of digital intervention in food waste. This design and research outcome serve as a robust foundation that can be directly utilized for the actual application development phase.
