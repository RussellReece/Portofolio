# Empact — Community Profile Website

## 📌 Introduction
Empact is a youth community organization dedicated to social impact and personal development. This project was developed in collaboration with a real client.

## 🎯 Objective
To convert a professionally designed Figma UI into a fully functional, highly responsive, and hosted community profile website within a one-month timeframe.

## 💡 Background / Why It Exists
The Empact Community required a digital presence to showcase their programs (such as Empower 360, Insight E, etc.), highlight their team profile, and share articles or event documentation. They needed a fast, accessible platform to reach a wider audience and support their youth empowerment activities.

## 🧑‍💼 My Role
As a **Front-End Developer** in the UKM Coding team, I was responsible for the design-to-code translation, implementing core pages, and ensuring a seamless browsing experience across all devices.

## ⚙️ Modules Created
Working alongside a 4-person front-end team, I designed and developed several key components:
*   **Core Pages:** Implemented and structured the Home, About, and Partners pages.
*   **Navigation Component:** Built the main navigation bar complete with an interactive mobile hamburger menu.
*   **Vercel Configuration:** Configured the deployment pipeline to ensure smooth releases on the Vercel platform.
*   **Quality Assurance:** Conducted cross-browser testing (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) and performed code reviews for my teammates' work.

## 🚀 Impact
This project successfully launched a high-performance, responsive community profile website. The platform now serves as Empact's official digital identity, actively supporting their social campaigns and streamlining youth empowerment program registrations.
