# KSFOOD — Manufacturing ERP & E-commerce

## 📌 Introduction
KSFOOD is a comprehensive case study project based on a real-world sauce manufacturing factory. This project spanned two academic semesters, integrating deep system analysis and full-stack development.

## 🎯 Objective
To design a complete and robust information system that unifies the factory's production, inventory, purchasing, and e-commerce sales channels into a single, cohesive platform.

## 💡 Background / Why It Exists
The sauce manufacturer required an integrated solution to handle both internal ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) operations and their external B2B/B2C e-commerce channels seamlessly. Previously disconnected systems led to inventory inconsistencies and operational inefficiencies across the supply chain.

## 🧑‍💼 My Role
As the **Team Lead, System Analyst & Front-End Developer**, I coordinated the group across two semesters. My responsibilities ranged from initial business process analysis and database architecture design to building the operational prototype interfaces.

## ⚙️ Modules Created
This project was divided into two main phases:
*   **Business Process Modeling (Semester 3):** Produced End-to-End process flow diagrams and comprehensive UML models (Use Case, Activity, and Sequence Diagrams).
*   **Front-End Prototype (Semester 3):** Built a Next.js B2C e-commerce storefront and a role-based ERP dashboard (Admin, Warehouse Staff, Sales).
*   **Database Architecture (Semester 4):** Designed the Conceptual, Logical, and Physical Data Models in MySQL. This included over 30 tables, composite key configurations, and automated stock update systems utilizing triggers and views.

## 🚀 Impact
The project successfully formulated a robust system blueprint and an interactive working prototype. The final outcome demonstrated that manufacturing operational needs can be effectively bridged with digital sales channels, offering real-time data visibility from raw material production to the end consumer.
