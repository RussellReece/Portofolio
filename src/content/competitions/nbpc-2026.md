# Participant | National Business Plan Competition (NBPC) 2 2026

## Overview

Represented team **Pioneering Strategists** at the **National Business Plan Competition (NBPC) #2 2026**, a nationally scoped competition attracting university teams from across Indonesia. We presented **Agilis**, our gamified health and lifestyle application — now in a significantly more refined and research-backed iteration compared to our earlier Logicodix 2025 entry.

## About Agilis (NBPC 2026 Iteration)

The NBPC 2026 version of **Agilis** represents a major evolution of the product concept, incorporating deeper market validation, a refined go-to-market strategy, and a more concrete technology roadmap:

> **Agilis** — *Merancang Ulang Gaya Hidup Sehat Melalui Integrasi Gamifikasi, Komunitas, dan Reward Nyata.*

### Key Evolution from Previous Iteration

- **Deeper Indonesian Market Research**: Primary surveys and interviews with target demographic (18–30 year olds in Tier 1–2 cities).
- **Validated Monetization Model**: Refined freemium model with partnership deals with local F&B and fitness brands as proof of concept.
- **Technical Architecture**: Detailed mobile app architecture using React Native + Firebase + ML recommendation engine.
- **Financial Projections**: 3-year P&L, cash flow statements, and break-even analysis based on realistic acquisition cost assumptions.

### Core Product Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| **Daily Mission System** | Streak-based daily health challenges with XP rewards |
| **Leaderboard & Guilds** | Community competition and accountability groups |
| **Reward Marketplace** | Real rewards from brand partners (gyms, F&B, supplements) |
| **AI Health Coach** | Personalized workout & nutrition plan generation |
| **Progress Analytics** | Visual health journey tracking with milestone badges |
