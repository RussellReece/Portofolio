# Semi-Finalist | Business Plan Competition GEM 2025

## Overview

Competed in the **Business Plan Competition GEM 2025** as part of team **Pioneering Strategists**. Our submission, **Eatventory**, is an AI-powered food inventory and demand-forecasting platform designed for small-to-medium food businesses and restaurants. The team reached the **Semi-Final round**, one of the competitive milestones in one of Indonesia's premier university entrepreneurship competitions.

## About Eatventory

> **Eatventory** — *Smart Meal Planning and Inventory Forecasting for the F&B Industry.*

Eatventory tackles one of the biggest pain points in the food & beverage industry: **food waste and stock-out events** caused by inaccurate demand forecasting. Key features include:

- **AI Demand Forecasting**: Predicts ingredient needs based on historical sales, weather, events, and trends.
- **Real-time Stock Monitoring**: Dashboard tracking current inventory levels with low-stock alerts.
- **Automated Purchase Orders**: Generates supplier POs automatically when stock hits reorder points.
- **Waste Analytics**: Reports on expired and over-ordered ingredients to optimize purchasing decisions.
- **Multi-outlet Support**: Centralized management for businesses with multiple branches.

## Business Model

Eatventory operates on a **SaaS Subscription** model:
- Tiered pricing based on number of SKUs and outlets managed.
- API integrations with existing POS systems (Moka, Majoo, etc.) for seamless data flow.
- Revenue from premium analytics dashboards and supplier marketplace referrals.
