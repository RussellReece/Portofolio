# Semi-Finalist | National Business Plan Competition (NBPC) 2025

## Overview

Reached the **Semi-Final round** of the **National Business Plan Competition (NBPC) 2025**. This achievement reflects the team's ability to develop and present a business plan in a nationally scoped competition.

The submission was prepared by **Pioneering Strategists** for the **Kesehatan & Lifestyle** subtheme. The proposal, titled **“Perancangan Aplikasi Gamifikasi ‘Agilis’ sebagai Solusi Membangun Gaya Hidup Sehat yang Menyenangkan,”** presents a digital health concept that turns everyday habits into an engaging game experience.

## About Agilis

Agilis encourages healthier routines such as drinking enough water, walking, working out, eating well, and getting sufficient sleep through:

- Health quests and challenges that users can complete progressively.
- Points, levels, and rankings to maintain motivation.
- Community interaction, mutual support, and friendly competition.
- Optional smartwatch integration for health activity tracking.
- Rewards that connect in-app progress with real-world benefits.

## Achievement

- Advanced to the **Semi-Final round** of NBPC 2025.
- Developed and presented a business plan with the **Pioneering Strategists** team.
- Proposed Agilis under the **Kesehatan & Lifestyle** subtheme.
