# Semi-Finalist | BPC Logicodix 2025

## Overview

Competed in the **Business Plan Competition (BPC) Logicodix 2025** representing team **Pioneering Strategists**. Our entry, **Agilis**, is a gamified health and lifestyle application that reimagines personal wellness through game mechanics, community features, and a real-reward ecosystem — designed to solve the widespread issue of low long-term exercise adherence.

The team successfully advanced to the **Semi-Final round**, competing against university teams from across Indonesia.

## About Agilis

> **Agilis** — *Merancang Ulang Gaya Hidup Sehat Melalui Integrasi Gamifikasi, Komunitas, dan Reward Nyata.*

Agilis addresses the problem that most health apps fail to keep users engaged after the first month. Our solution integrates:

- **Gamification Engine**: XP points, level-up system, achievement badges, daily challenges, and leaderboards.
- **Community Hub**: Social feeds, accountability groups, and challenge matchmaking.
- **Real Reward System**: Points redeemable for tangible rewards from partner brands (gyms, healthy F&B, supplements).
- **Personalized AI Coach**: Adaptive workout plans and nutrition suggestions based on user data.

## Business Model

Agilis follows a **Freemium + B2B Partnership** model:
- Free tier with core gamification features.
- Premium subscription for advanced analytics, AI coaching, and exclusive challenges.
- Revenue sharing with brand partners who sponsor challenges and redeem rewards.
