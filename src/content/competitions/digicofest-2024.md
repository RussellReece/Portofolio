# Semi-Finalist | Business Case Competition DIGICOFEST 2024

## Overview

Competed in the **Business Case Competition DIGICOFEST 2024**, a prestigious business case competition where teams are given a real-world corporate challenge and must develop and present a data-driven strategic recommendation. Our team advanced to the **Semi-Final round** with our case analysis for **CIS PDAM Surabaya**, Surabaya's regional water utility company.

## The Case: CIS PDAM Surabaya

The case centered on **CIS (Customer Information System) PDAM Surabaya**, a digital service platform for the water utility. The core challenge was:

> *How can PDAM Surabaya significantly increase digital adoption and brand awareness for its CIS platform among Surabaya citizens who still rely on manual, offline service channels?*

## Our Strategy

Our team's solution focused on a **multi-channel digital transformation strategy** with three core pillars:

### 1. Feature Enhancement
- **Self-Service Bill Payment & History**: Streamlined in-app payment with integrated e-wallet support (GoPay, OVO, ShopeePay).
- **Smart Leak Reporting**: AI-assisted photo submission for pipe leak reports with real-time status tracking.
- **Usage Analytics Dashboard**: Personal water consumption insights with conservation tips.

### 2. Community-Driven Awareness
- **"Surabaya Hemat Air" Campaign**: A gamified challenge encouraging citizens to reduce water consumption with leaderboards and neighborhood rankings.
- **Partnership with RT/RW**: Leveraging local community leaders as digital ambassadors to promote app adoption in neighborhoods.

### 3. Omnichannel Integration
- **WhatsApp Bot Integration**: Service requests and bill notifications via WhatsApp for non-smartphone users.
- **Physical QR Code Placement**: QR codes at PDAM offices, water meter inspection notices, and partner locations.
