# Participant | ICT Recursion 2025

## Overview

Competed in **ICT Recursion 2025** as part of team **Pioneering Strategists**. ICT Recursion is a business-focused technology competition that challenges student teams to develop viable digital business solutions. This competition served as one of the **formative early experiences** for the Pioneering Strategists team, where core team chemistry and the initial Agilis concept were refined before future competitions.

## Context & Significance

ICT Recursion 2025 was a critical stepping stone:
- It was one of the **first major competitions** entered by the Pioneering Strategists team.
- The competition experience informed the team's presentation style, proposal structure, and business model thinking for subsequent competitions (GEM 2025, Logicodix 2025, NBPC 2026).
- The Business Model Canvas and pitch narrative developed here were later iterated upon for more polished submissions.

## Our Submission

The team presented a **business technology innovation** aligned with the competition's digital transformation theme, developing a proposal around the concept of health-tech gamification — which would later mature into **Agilis**.

The submission included:
- **Business Model Canvas (BMC)** outlining the value proposition, customer segments, revenue streams, and cost structure.
- A **Proposal Document** detailing the problem, solution, market opportunity, and implementation plan.
