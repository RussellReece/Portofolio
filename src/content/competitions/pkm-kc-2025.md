# Participant | PKM-KC 2025

## Overview

Participated in the **Program Kreativitas Mahasiswa — Karsa Cipta (PKM-KC) 2025**, the Indonesian national student creativity program organized by **Kemendikbudristek** (Ministry of Education, Culture, Research, and Technology). PKM-KC focuses on creating innovative technical products or prototypes that solve real-world problems and contribute to society.

This participation involved a rigorous proposal writing process, coordinating with supervisors, and managing all required submission documentation through the official **SIMBELMAWA** portal.

## About PKM-KC

PKM (Program Kreativitas Mahasiswa) is one of Indonesia's most prestigious national academic competitions for university students. The **Karsa Cipta (KC)** category specifically requires teams to create a **functional prototype or technological innovation** — not just a business plan, but an actual working system or tool.

**Key Requirements:**
- A full technical proposal following the official Kemendikbudristek template.
- Supervisor biodata and institutional endorsement letters.
- Detailed methodology, budget plan (RAB), and activity timeline.
- A functional prototype as the end deliverable.
